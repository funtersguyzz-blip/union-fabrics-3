Four product divisions, built on one supply chain.

Every division below draws on the same greige weaving, dyeing, printing and finishing lines — so construction, colour and quality stay consistent whichever category you order from.

Home & Utility Bedding

Sheeting, percale, sateen and dobby constructions for flat sheets, fitted sheets, shams, duvet covers and utility cloth, in thread counts from T-120 through T-1000.

Percale

Crisp, cool-hand weave

A tight plain weave for sheets and shams that stays crisp wash after wash.

Sateen

Lustrous, soft-hand weave

A four-over, one-under weave with a soft drape and subtle sheen, up to T-1000.

Dobby & Sheeting

Structured and everyday cloth

Woven-in textures for structured lines, plus mid-count sheeting for everyday bedding programs.

Fabrics

Greige, dyed, printed and laminated fabrics supplying mattress, upholstery, healthcare and technical end-uses, digitally or rotary printed to order.

Digital printing

Short-run, high-definition colourways for custom and fast-turn orders.

Rotary printing

High-volume repeat patterns for standing collections and program business.

Lamination

Bonded and laminated constructions for mattress ticking and technical fabric applications.

Denim Division

Our ESG Story

Sustainability, built into the process — not added after it.

Environmentally considered systems have run through Union Fabrics' manufacturing for more than two decades: from a dedicated organic cotton line to water- and chemical-conscious dyeing on the denim floor.

Fibres we work with

A sustainable-materials range sits alongside our conventional cotton lines, run through the same finishing equipment.

GOTS Organic Cotton

Certified organic fibre with traceability from field to finished roll.

Hemp

A lower-input natural fibre blended into select bedding and fabric lines.

Bamboo

Soft-hand, fast-growing fibre used across select home-textile programs.

Tencel & Recycled Polyester

Regenerated and recycled fibres offered for around- and above-mattress applications.

Where sustainability shows up on the floor

Certification is treated as a production discipline, audited specifically across the organic cotton and denim lines.

01

Water & dye recovery

Recovery processes built into indigo dyeing to reduce water and chemical use on the denim floor.

02

Certified organic supply

A GOTS-certified line keeps organic cotton traceable and segregated through every finishing stage.

03

Energy-conscious operations

Efficiency upgrades across weaving, dyeing and finishing equipment reduce energy intensity per metre produced.

Standards we work to

Certification is audited against our financial and operational records, not just claimed.

Our vision and mission

Two statements that guide how the mill grows and who it grows for.

Vision

To be the supplier the world's leading home-textile and apparel brands turn to first — for consistency, traceability and scale, from a single Karachi campus.

Mission

To keep every stage of production — weaving, dyeing, printing, finishing and stitching — under one roof, so quality, lead times and sustainability commitments stay in our control.
